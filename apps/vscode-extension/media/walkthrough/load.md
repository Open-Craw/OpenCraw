# Load what you want to read

In Studio press **Load** and choose:

- **Web page**: paste an address. Cookie banners are closed for you.
- **Document**: a PDF, a spreadsheet (xlsx, csv), a deck, a Word file, JSON, YAML, XML or Markdown.

Whatever you load appears on the left, the way it looks: a page, a PDF page, a grid or a tree. The steps of the recipe are on the right.

Big files are fine: a long spreadsheet or JSON shows only the part you are looking at.
